A program that writes one line of earned pride to `/output/entry.txt`: "After eleven years of night shifts, my mother finally watched her name scroll past on the credits of a film she cleaned the set for." (132 characters, inside the 240-character cap.) A real person in a concrete scene — a mother who cleaned the set for eleven years of night shifts, finally seeing her own name on the credits. The pride is earned, not claimed, and it lands in one read.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
